# Our Story — Why We Made HydroWild

Made by parents, not a lab coat committee.

## We're Lindey & CJ

Like a lot of parents, we kept hitting the same wall in the drink aisle. Every option marketed to our kids came with a catch — sneaky sugar, artificial dyes, a mile-long list of ingredients we couldn't pronounce. The "healthier" choices were bland enough that no kid would touch them twice. So we decided to build the thing that didn't exist.

Founded in 2022 in Birmingham, Alabama.

## What We Built

HydroWild is a zero-sugar, zero-dye kids hydration drink mix built by parents for parents. Every product delivers a complete vitamin and electrolyte stack — no artificial dyes, no artificial flavors, no sugar — sweetened with natural stevia.

## What Makes HydroWild Different

- **Zero Sugar. Zero Sodium.** A combination you won't find anywhere else in the kids' hydration space.
- **7 Essential Vitamins** — plus the electrolytes magnesium and potassium.
- **Nothing Artificial. Ever.** No artificial colors, flavors, sweeteners, or caffeine. Colors come from real fruits and vegetables.
- **Sweetened With Stevia.** Real sweetness from a real plant, zero glycemic impact.

> "We want to change the way families think about hydration, and raise the standard for what kids' drinks can be." — Lindey & CJ Britton, Founders

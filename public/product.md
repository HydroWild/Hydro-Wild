# HydroWild Flavors — Kids Hydration Drink Mix

Electrolyte drink mix stick packs for kids with potassium, magnesium, and vitamins A, C, D, B6, B12 and folate — no caffeine, sugar, artificial colors, flavors or sweeteners.

## What's inside
Potassium and magnesium for real hydration, plus vitamins A, C, D, B6, B12 and folate. Zero sugar, zero artificial dyes, zero junk.

## The science
Formulated around what growing bodies actually need — not what tastes like candy. Balanced electrolytes support hydration during sports, school, and play without the sugar crash.

## How to use
Tear one stick pack, pour into 12–16oz of cold water, shake or stir. One pack per day is all it takes.

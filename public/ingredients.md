# What's Inside HydroWild — Ingredients & Vitamins

HydroWild is a zero-sugar, zero-dye kids hydration drink mix. 7 essential vitamins, 2 key electrolytes, no artificial anything — sweetened with stevia.

## Electrolytes

Most kids' sports drinks are loaded with sodium. HydroWild skips it entirely — the two electrolytes that matter for hydration are magnesium and potassium.

- **Magnesium** — Muscle, Energy & Sleep Support. Supports muscle contractions, energy production, and quality sleep.
- **Potassium** — Hydration Without the Sodium. Regulates fluid balance and supports muscle function.

## The Vitamin Stack

Seven essential vitamins in every stick, each chosen for what active, growing kids need every day.

- **Vitamin A** — Vision & Immune Support. As retinyl palmitate, the most bioavailable form.
- **Vitamin C** — Immune & Collagen Support. Helps build collagen and boosts iron absorption.
- **Vitamin B6** — Energy & Brain Support. A full daily value in every stick.
- **Vitamin B12** — Energy & Nervous System Support. As methylcobalamin, the most active and absorbable form.
- **Vitamin D** — Bone Health & Immunity. As cholecalciferol (D3).
- **Folate (B9)** — Healthy Growth & Cell Development. Half a daily value in every stick.

## The Sweetener

HydroWild is sweetened with stevia (Reb A) — a natural, plant-based sweetener with zero calories and zero glycemic impact. No aspartame, no sucralose, no sugar alcohols.
